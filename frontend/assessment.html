In a relational database, what is the key behavioral difference between RANK() and DENSE_RANK() when duplicate values are encountered in the ORDER BY clause?

🧠 Explainable AI Diagnostic Notes:

Correct Answer: A. RANK() increments the ranking counter by the number of ties (leaving gaps like 1, 2, 2, 4), whereas DENSE_RANK() continues strictly sequentially (1, 2, 2, 3).
